INVITATION TO TENDER
Renovation and Energy-Efficiency Upgrade of Municipal Office Building
Tender Reference: MUN-BLD-2026-041
Contracting authority: Coastal Municipal Development Agency (CMDA)
Publication: 8 September 2026
Submission deadline: 6 October 2026 at 10:30
Contract period: 120 calendar days
Estimated value: MAD 1,250,000 excluding VAT
Procedure: Open competitive procedure
1. Project Scope
The authority intends to renovate a four-storey municipal office building and improve its energy performance. Works
include roof insulation, selected window replacement, LED lighting, HVAC improvements, electrical adjustments,
painting, commissioning, testing and handover.
2. Eligibility and Administrative Requirements
• E-01 The bidder must be legally registered to perform building renovation and related electrical/HVAC works.
• E-02 The bidder must provide valid tax-clearance and social-security compliance certificates.
• E-03 The bidder must submit a signed declaration of honour using the prescribed form.
• E-04 The bidder must provide a provisional tender guarantee of MAD 25,000.
• E-05 The bidder must demonstrate at least 4 comparable building renovation projects completed during the
previous 6 years, including client references.

3. Technical Requirements
• T-01 Roof insulation must achieve a minimum thermal resistance of R = 5.0 m²K/W.
• T-02 Replacement windows must use double glazing with a declared U-value not exceeding 1.6 W/m²K.
• T-03 All replacement lighting fixtures must be LED with a minimum efficacy of 120 lm/W.
• T-04 HVAC units must use refrigerant with a global-warming-potential value below 750 and include programmable
controls.
• T-05 The bidder must provide electrical installation test certificates after completion.
• T-06 The contractor must complete commissioning and provide as-built drawings and maintenance documentation.
• T-07 The works must be completed within 120 calendar days from contractual commencement.
• T-08 The contractor must provide a minimum 24-month defects liability/warranty period.
4. Commercial and Contractual Conditions
• The contract price shall remain firm and non-revisable.
• A performance guarantee equal to 10% of the awarded contract amount shall be provided by the successful
contractor.
• Payment will be made through monthly certified progress statements, subject to approval and contractual retention
provisions.
• Delay penalties may be applied at 1/1000 of the contract amount per calendar day, subject to the contractual cap.

5. Evaluation Method
• Technical quality: 45%
• Price: 35%
• Relevant experience: 10%
• Work methodology and schedule: 10%
Evaluation weights are assessment criteria, not additional bidder requirements.
6. Submission Documents
• Administrative certificates and declaration required by Section 2.
• Technical proposal and methodology.
• Detailed bill of quantities and financial offer.
• Project schedule and work programme.
• Product technical datasheets where applicable.
7. Clarifications
Requests for clarification may be submitted up to 8 calendar days before the tender deadline. The authority may request
clarification but will not accept a material change to the offer.
The deadline is procedural information and must not be interpreted as a bidder qualification requirement.

8. Conditional Technical Requirement
• T-09 (conditional) If the bidder proposes replacing HVAC equipment rather than repairing existing units, the proposed
replacement units must include manufacturer documentation confirming the required refrigerant specification and
programmable control capability.
9. Repeated References and Cross-References
The following statements repeat or summarize obligations already defined above. They do not create additional
requirements:
• The tender guarantee remains MAD 25,000; a second reference is the same guarantee obligation.
• The four-project experience condition remains the same eligibility requirement even when referenced elsewhere.
• The 24-month defects liability period remains one warranty obligation.
• The 120-day completion period is one contractual performance obligation even when repeated in the schedule.
10. Site and Project Facts — Informational
• The building has approximately 180 employees.
• The roof area is approximately 1,900 m².
• The building was originally constructed in 1998.
• The authority expects works while parts of the building remain occupied.

11. Reviewer Verification Examples — Not Requirements
The examples below describe checks that a procurement reviewer may perform. They are not additional bidder obligations.
• Example A: verify that each experience reference is dated within the six-year period.
• Example B: verify the declared U-value in the window manufacturer's datasheet.
• Example C: verify the tender guarantee amount against the submitted bank instrument.
• Example D: verify the HVAC refrigerant declaration when the conditional requirement applies.
• Example E: verify commissioning documents before acceptance.

13. Additional Contract Details
• The contractor is responsible for site safety, temporary protection and removal of construction waste generated by the
works.
• The contractor shall maintain appropriate insurance coverage required by applicable law and the contract.
• Imported materials remain the contractor's responsibility until delivered and accepted at the project site.
Final Tender Statement
Only compliant offers satisfying applicable eligibility, technical, contractual and submission conditions will proceed to
final evaluation. The authority may reject non-compliant offers in accordance with procurement rules.

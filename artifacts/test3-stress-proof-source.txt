REQUEST FOR TENDER
Supply, Installation and Maintenance of a Network & Access-Control System
Tender Reference: RFT-SEC-2026-027
Procuring entity North Atlas Business Center (NABC)
Publication date 4 September 2026
Submission deadline 28 September 2026 at 11:00
Contract duration 90 calendar days
Estimated value MAD 420,000 excluding VAT
Method Open competitive tender
1. Scope
The contracting authority seeks a qualified supplier to supply, install, configure, test and maintain a network
infrastructure and electronic access-control system for a three-floor business facility. The scope includes switches,
wireless access points, firewall equipment, access-control readers, controllers, cabling, configuration, commissioning,
documentation and staff handover.
2. Mandatory Administrative and Eligibility Requirements
• R-01 The bidder must be legally registered and authorized to perform the contracted activities.
• R-02 The bidder must provide a valid tax-clearance certificate and a valid social-security compliance certificate at the
time of submission.
• R-03 The bidder must submit a signed tender declaration using the form included in this tender dossier.
• R-04 The bidder must provide a provisional bid security of MAD 12,000.
• R-05 The bidder must demonstrate at least 3 completed comparable projects during the last 5 years, with
documentary references.

3. Technical Requirements
• R-06 The proposed firewall must support at least 1 Gbps stateful throughput and include active security subscriptions
for the first 12 months.
• R-07 The wireless solution must provide Wi-Fi 6 access points with centralized management and support a minimum
of 120 concurrent clients per access point.
• R-08 The access-control system must support at least 16 controlled doors, RFID credentials, event logging and a
central administration console.
• R-09 The bidder must supply and install Category 6A structured cabling and provide certification test results for all
installed links.
• R-10 The bidder must provide a minimum 24-month warranty covering supplied hardware and installation
workmanship.
• R-11 The bidder must provide on-site corrective support within 24 hours after a covered critical incident is reported.
• R-12 The bidder must submit a detailed implementation schedule and commissioning plan before contract award.
4. Commercial and Contractual Conditions
• Prices shall remain firm and non-revisable throughout the contract.
• Payment will be made after delivery, installation, successful commissioning, provisional acceptance and compliant
invoices.
• A performance guarantee equal to 10% of the contract value shall be provided by the successful bidder.
• The supplier remains responsible for imported equipment, freight, customs and delivery until acceptance at the site.
• Late-delivery penalties may apply at 1/1000 of the contract amount per calendar day, capped at 8%.

5. Clarifications and Submission Procedure
Questions must be submitted no later than 7 calendar days before the submission deadline. The authority may request
clarification, but a clarification may not materially change the substance of the bid.
The submission deadline is 28 September 2026 at 11:00. This is a procedural deadline and is not itself a bidder
qualification requirement.
6. Evaluation Criteria
• Technical compliance: 40%
• Price: 35%
• Relevant experience: 15%
• Implementation methodology: 10%
These percentages describe evaluation only. They are not bidder requirements and must not become separate
compliance requirements.
7. Required Submission Package
• Administrative eligibility documents listed in Section 2.
• Technical proposal addressing the technical requirements.
• Equipment datasheets and manufacturer documentation.
• Implementation schedule and commissioning plan.
• Financial offer using the prescribed price schedule.
Analysis note: distinguish procedural information, evaluation weights, deadlines and document lists from substantive
bidder obligations. Do not count headings or explanatory notes as requirements.

second requirement.
